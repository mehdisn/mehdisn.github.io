abstract: "Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) is a crucial domain within research and industry, aiming to develop AI models that provide human-understandable explanations for their decisions. While the challenges in AI, deep learning, and big data have been extensively explored, the specific concerns of XAI developers have received limited attention. To address this gap, we analysed discussions on Stack Exchange websites to delve into these issues. Through a combination of automated and Manual analysis, we identified 6 overarching categories, 10 distinct topics, and 40 sub-topics commonly discussed by developers."
